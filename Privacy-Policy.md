# Privacy Policy

At **Starlight Dev**, we are committed to protecting user privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information of users of our Discord bot.

## 1. Information We Collect

1.1. **User Information**: When you add our bot to your server, we may collect information such as your Discord username, user ID, and server settings.  
1.2. **Usage Data**: We may collect information about how you interact with the bot, such as command usage.  

## 2. How We Use Your Information

2.1. **Bot Improvement**: We use the collected data to improve the features and performance of the Discord bot, such as optimizing bot usage and enhancing user experience.
2.3. **Legal Compliance**: We may use collected information to comply with relevant laws, regulations, and legal requests.  
2.4. **AI Chat Functionality**: We use the content of your messages in AI Chat to generate appropriate responses and improve the AI's understanding and performance.

## 3. Data Retention and Security

3.1. **Data Retention**: We will retain user data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.  
3.2. **Data Security**: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information we collect from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## 4. Your Rights and Choices

4.1. **Access and Correction**: You have the right to access, update, or correct the personal information we have about you.  
4.2. **Deletion**: You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal or operational requirements.  
4.3. **Opt-Out**: If you no longer want us to collect or share your information with third-party services, you can opt-out by removing the bot from your Discord server.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

5.1. We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy.

## 6. Contact Us

6.1. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us on Discord.

---

**By using our Discord bot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this privacy policy.**
